# Safety

> **Permission status.** Written permission from Funcom has been requested and is pending. A follow-up was sent on 24 September 2026 (support ticket #308630). No agent will play in Dune: Awakening until a written answer is received. If the answer is no, the agents stay out. If the answer is yes with conditions, those conditions are followed. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Funcom.

This folder is organized with the four functions of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0): Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. The framework is voluntary. Using its headings is not a claim of certification. Citation: [../docs/references.md](../docs/references.md).

## Govern

The permission request is the authority for anything that would happen inside Dune: Awakening. Funcom may refuse, pause, or end the exception. The project will not claim Funcom's endorsement unless Funcom chooses it. A human operator is required for every session. Details: [../ethics/oversight.md](../ethics/oversight.md).

## Map

The work that exists today is the mock survival world. The work that has been asked for, and not yet allowed, is two agents on one closed, self-hosted, invite-only, passworded, unlisted battlegroup. Official servers and shared public or rented private-server Worlds are out of the request. The boundary is drawn in [boundaries.md](boundaries.md) and [closed-server.md](closed-server.md).

## Measure

The sandbox metrics are defined in [../methodology/evaluation.md](../methodology/evaluation.md). They measure planning, handoff, and recovery in the mock world. They do not measure farm rate or rank. A game measurement plan is not registered, because no game sessions are allowed.

## Manage

Stops are defined in [stop-procedures.md](stop-procedures.md) and [../ethics/incidents.md](../ethics/incidents.md). The sandbox kill switch and approval gate are implemented. Using them on a battlegroup is **proposed, pending Funcom review**.
