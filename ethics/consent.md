# Consent and briefing

**Proposed, pending Funcom review.** The September 2026 request does not specify how people are invited, what they are told, or how consent is recorded. No participants are being recruited. The text below is a draft to review, not a form in use.

## Who would be asked

Only people in the research group on the single closed battlegroup, and only after written permission to run agents at all. The size of that group is an open question. This draft does not set a number and does not describe an invitation channel.

People outside the research group are not participants. The request already says the agents do not contact players outside the group in the Deep Desert or social hubs. There is no consent path that widens that boundary.

## What a briefing would cover

Before someone shares a session with the agents, the operator would tell them, in plain language:

- who is running the study, and that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Funcom
- that permission, if it exists by then, is limited, revocable, and noncommercial
- that two agents, Eve and Edos, will act while a human operator is present
- that the session can be stopped by the operator or if Funcom ends the exception
- what is written to the log, using the contents agreed in the protocol
- that they can decline, and that declining has no effect on their access to the public game, which this project does not control
- how to reach the operator (doschott@gmail.com) with a question or a complaint

A script that matches the final protocol would be stored in this folder before the first session. It is not written yet, because the protocol would have to include Funcom's conditions.

## What a consent record would hold

**Proposed.** A record outside the agent JSONL, stored with the operator, containing:

- the date
- a statement that the person received the briefing and agreed
- the protocol version they were shown
- a role label used in notes

The record would not need a copy of a government identifier. Whether a real name is required is part of the open question on personal data. See [../methodology/data-handling.md](../methodology/data-handling.md).

Agreement to be in the room is not agreement to publish a person's name. The public writeup proposed here uses role labels unless someone asks to be named and the operator agrees.

## Declining and leaving

**Proposed.** A person can decline before a session or leave during one. The operator stops the agents if the only remaining human arrangement no longer matches what the person agreed to. Leaving is recorded as a stop in the research notes (date and session id), not as a failure of the agent.

## Compensation

The request is silent. **Proposed:** no compensation is promised. If any payment or in-game help were later considered, it would be written down first and checked against the request's ban on boosting and real-money trading. This draft does not offer either.

## Minors

**Proposed:** the research group is adults. The project will not recruit minors. The request does not discuss age. This proposal is the conservative default until a written answer says otherwise.

## Ethics review

Whether an institutional review board or another review is required is an open question. No approval is claimed.
